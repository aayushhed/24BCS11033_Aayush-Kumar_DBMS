### Problem - Non-Correlated Subquery

Find all dishes whose cost is greater than the **average cost of all dishes** in the `food` table.

Display:
- `f_name`
- `f_cost`
- `f_type`

Use a **non-correlated subquery** to calculate the overall average cost.

**Hint:** Use `AVG()` inside a subquery on the `food` table.
